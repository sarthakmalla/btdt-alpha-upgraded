FAQ

Everything you need to know

Does BTDT provide visas or act as a visa agency?

No. BTDT is strictly a peer-to-peer network connecting incoming students with Verified Alumni and Been There, Done That Mentors from their home country. Mentors share practical do's & don'ts from lived experience — we never provide, process, or file visas. A dedicated guided document-walkthrough service is planned for the future.

Why is BTDT text-first instead of video calls?

Text-first sessions give you permanent written checklists, verified campus links, and do's & don'ts notes you can reference anytime without relying on scattered forums. Audio/video calls are planned for v2.

How are mentors verified and how is Aura calculated?

Every peer is verified using their Student ID card, official enrollment documents, or Alumni Diploma. Aura (/100) aggregates Accuracy, Response Speed, and Clarity from completed paid sessions, and negative reviews are never hidden.

How do bookings and Boarding Passes work?

Pick a countryman, choose your text-first session (€20, €100, or €100–€120), and receive a digital Boarding Pass (#BTDT-XXXX) synced directly to your Wallet with all saved notes.

What does the +€40 Airport Meet & Greet include?

Your mentor meets you at the arrivals terminal and escorts you to your dorm or apartment via local public transit, Uber, or Bolt. All transit and ride fares are paid directly by the incoming student.

Can my university enroll or partner with BTDT?

Yes. Any university worldwide can join via Enroll University in our Directory or Footer, immediately opening chapters across all 14 home countries.
